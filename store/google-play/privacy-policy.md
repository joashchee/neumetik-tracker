# Neumetik Tracker: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Neumetik Tracker is made by ansiapps. This policy covers the Neumetik
Tracker app on Android, iOS, the desktop and the web.

## What we collect

Nothing. Neumetik Tracker has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics
and no crash reporting. It doesn't send any information about you, your
device or your use of it to us or to anyone else.

## Your files

The songs and samples you open, edit, save and export stay on your
device. The app reads a file only when you choose it with your device's
file picker, and writes a file only where you choose to save it.

## Permissions

The app uses your device's sound output to play music. If you connect a
MIDI keyboard, the app reads the notes you play on it to enter them
into the song. Nothing you play is recorded or sent anywhere.

## The web version

The web version runs entirely in your browser. It is served by GitHub
Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under
GitHub's own privacy policy.

## What the app remembers

The app keeps its own settings (such as the octave, interpolation and
whether the pattern follows the song) on your device, and on the web
in your browser's local storage, so they're there next time. They never
leave your device.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at
